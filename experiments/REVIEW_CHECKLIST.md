# Author review milestone

This milestone deliberately precedes the twelve-artifact campaign.

Review the compiled manuscript together with raw evidence, rather than approving
an abstract plan to run a much larger matrix.

- Confirm the scientific/execution distinction and the limited readiness claim.
- Inspect MapCoder provenance, HumanEval-only patch, local model substitution,
  prompts/outputs, restricted test evaluator and the separation of task correctness
  from valid experiment-output structure.
- Inspect the before/after readiness decisions, complete attempt ledger, frozen
  identities and Slurm accounting. Failed or canceled submissions must remain
  visible.
- Confirm that no synthetic count is described as an independent study, a natural
  failure rate, a reproduced paper result or measured GPU savings.
- Review the current candidate corpus for coverage beyond code generation/repair,
  and decide which paper-compatible model and sandbox configurations are eligible.
- Agree on campaign budgets, comparison conditions, failure penalties, equivalence
  margins, artifact-level analysis, and independent fault design before scaling.
- Complete author declarations, permanent archive metadata and licensing review
  before journal submission or public artifact release.

The controller conformance suite and small CUDA integration pilot are executed.
The research-artifact pilot status is recorded in the generated manuscript and
run-status files. The twelve-artifact campaign remains prospective.
